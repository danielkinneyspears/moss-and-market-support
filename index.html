Sending a problem report

  1. Open Settings in the game.
  2. Tap Prepare report, then Share report.
  3. Send it to the address above.

The report holds no names and none of your towns' contents. What it holds.

Purchases

Town levels 1 and 2 are free. The full game is one purchase and opens every level after that, and the seasonal packs are sold on their own. If a purchase is missing on a new phone, open the full game's page from the menu and tap Restore Purchases, signed in to the same Apple Account.

Refunds go through Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Saves

Towns are saved on the phone as you play. To move a town to another phone, export it from Your towns and import the file on the other phone.
