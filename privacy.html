Moss & Market

Privacy

Moss & Market does not collect your personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your towns

Your towns are saved on your iPhone. If you export a backup from Your towns, the file goes wherever you send it. Deleting the app deletes the towns on the phone.

Purchases

The full game and the seasonal packs are sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment, and we never see your card or your Apple Account. The game checks your purchases on the phone with Apple's StoreKit, so it knows what you own.

The problem journal

Settings has a journal that keeps a small record on your iPhone to help with bug reports: actions, coin changes, progress, save problems and performance. It holds no names and none of your towns' contents. It keeps up to 14 days or 1,200 entries, it is on by default, and you can turn it off or clear it in Settings.

The journal never uploads anything by itself. It leaves your phone only if you prepare a report and share it, and then it goes only where you send it.

Children

The game collects nothing from anyone, children included.
